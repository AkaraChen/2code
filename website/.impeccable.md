## Design Context

### Users
Independent developers and hackers who use AI coding tools daily and want a calmer, more durable working environment. They are usually juggling active branches, terminal-heavy workflows, and ongoing conversations with AI assistants, and they want to keep momentum without reconstructing project context every time they switch tasks.

### Brand Personality
Sharp, ambitious, calm-under-pressure. 2code should feel like a serious tool for focused builders: confident without hype, technical without looking cold, and expressive without drifting into flashy AI clichés.

### Aesthetic Direction
An annotated engineering datasheet on warm paper. The site should read as one meticulously typeset technical document rather than a template SaaS page: hairline rules do the structure, a four-step ink scale does the hierarchy, and a squarish slab display face (Zilla Slab) sits over tightly-tracked Inter with Geist Mono for labels, captions and keycaps. Warm greige/cream surfaces in light, warm greenish-brown blacks in dark — never neutral gray, never neon sci-fi.

Color is rationed on purpose. The chartreuse marker (`--marker`) is the only loud element and behaves like a physical highlighter pen: swiped behind small mono labels, never used as a button or panel fill. The one other accent is the green agent-status dot borrowed from the app.

### Design Principles
Preserve flow by keeping the interface legible, calm, and structurally clear.
Show system depth through composition and narrative, not generic feature-card repetition.
Keep the human collaborator centered even when presenting AI capabilities.
Use warm, tactile visual language to differentiate 2code from common AI-startup aesthetics.
Let the product be its own hero image: real screenshots carry the page, and no decorative stock art stands in for the app.
Match the product's own restraint — the site and the app should look like the same piece of software.
Stay keyboard-first: every primary CTA advertises its shortcut with a keycap, as the audience expects.

### Non-negotiables
- Corners are 2px. No pill shapes, no glassmorphism, no gradient fills, no glow.
- Borders and surface stepping carry depth; shadows are a whisper at most.
- Light and dark are equal citizens. Dark follows the system unless the visitor overrides it.
- Claims on the page must be verifiable today. No invented metrics, stars, or user counts.
